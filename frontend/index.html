Architecture & Methodology

How FRIDAY Detects Scams

Unlike blunt blacklists that fail against new fake companies, FRIDAY combines behavioral linguistics, known fraud vectors, and the Gemini API.

01 // INGESTION

Text Normalization

Pasted job descriptions, emails, and recruiter messages are sanitized, stripped of script tags, and extracted into behavioral vectors.

02 // PATTERN MATCHING

Multi-Vector Analysis

The text is cross-referenced against fraud vectors: payment requests, fake check equipment scams, encrypted messaging pivots, and wage-to-skill disparity.

03 // GEMINI REASONING

Cognitive Assessment

Gemini 2.5 performs deep contextual reasoning, isolating exact quotes, identifying contradictions, and balancing positive corporate signals.

04 // EXPLAINABLE REPORT

Actionable Defense

The candidate receives an explainable 0–100 risk score, an itemized evidence breakdown, and clear safety steps before any harm occurs.

Cyber Intelligence Catalog

Common Employment Scam Vectors

Learn the standard tactics cybercriminals and fake recruiters deploy against students and job seekers.

Upfront Fees & Training

Demands for "background check fees", "training certification fees", or "onboarding deposits". Legitimate employers pay you—never the other way around.

Fake Check Equipment Scam

The "employer" sends a counterfeit electronic check to purchase a home office laptop from an "approved vendor". When the check bounces, you lose real money.

Telegram / WhatsApp Pivots

Interview requests conducted exclusively over personal messaging apps or encrypted chats without video or verifiable corporate emails.

Unrealistic Compensation

Promising $60–$90/hr for entry-level data entry, app optimization, or envelope stuffing without rigorous technical or portfolio evaluation.

Premature Identity Theft

Soliciting Social Security Numbers, banking routing details, passport copies, or crypto addresses before formal written offers and contract signing.

Urgency & Pressure Tactics

"Hired immediately with no interview", "Start within 2 hours", or high-pressure tactics engineered to bypass normal background scrutiny.

Candidate Defense Checklist

Golden Rules of Job Safety

Follow these baseline cybersecurity practices to protect your identity, bank account, and time.

1. Check Corporate Domains

Legitimate recruiters contact you from official corporate domains (e.g. recruiter@google.com), not generic gmail.com or outlook.com accounts.

2. Verify on Careers Page

Navigate directly to the employer's official website without clicking links in unsolicited messages. Confirm the exact Job ID is officially open.

3. Never Accept Check For Equipment

Real employers provide pre-configured equipment directly from corporate IT or a trusted vendor. They never ask you to deposit a check and wire surplus funds.

4. Insist on Face-to-Face Video

Professional organizations conduct interviews via Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, or Zoom with camera on. Never accept a text-only interview on Telegram.
